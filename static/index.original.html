About the 3D ULPIN Extension

The prototype retains the parent ULPIN as the base parcel identity and represents vertical, underground, elevated and air-right spaces as distinct 3D cadastral objects.

12345678901234-F03-V-PRV-A301-V01

Interoperability

Designed for API-based integration and geospatial exchange including GeoJSON, CityGML/3D city models, IFC/BIM, LAS/LAZ and 3D Tiles.
